SENTINEL / KINETIC CORE
Implementation brief and reusable authoring prompt

Build the existing Sentinel administrator login and observability dashboard around an original, precision-machined 3D instrument using Three.js. The desired character is a cinematic mechanical transformation combined with the restrained finish of a high-end watch movement. The instrument must feel assembled from manufactured parts. Keep the application's Korean typography and working authentication and telemetry.

Art direction
Use cool satin titanium, small polished edge highlights, graphite ceramic inserts, subdued brass gears and a blue-white central optic. Distinguish materials through roughness, reflection and surface detail. Give parts real thickness, bevels, recesses, socket heads, fine fluting and machining grooves. Compose the mechanism as the focal object with open space around it. Supporting controls should feel quiet and deliberate.

Geometry and motion
Model the chassis, exposed gear carrier, spoke rotor, segmented armor, iris plates and optic as independent meshes. Give each layer an assembled pose and a deliberately authored separation path. Unlock the outer sections, extend the inner layers, open the iris and reveal the optic. Use staggered acceleration and deceleration with a readable pause, then reverse the sequence without a loop seam. Counter-rotate gear groups. Articulated rods should follow both moving anchors. Provide automatic, exploded and assembled views, plus a persistent motion toggle.

Lighting and camera
Use physically based metallic materials and a filtered studio environment. Large softbox reflections should reveal edge curvature. Preserve dark recesses and inter-part shadows without crushing the entire model to black or bleaching the metal white. Frame the full extended assembly with a perspective camera and restrained pointer response. Keep the object clear at the actual login and dashboard sizes; inspect rendered screenshots after changes.

Engineering constraints
Self-host the pinned Three.js runtime and license. Preserve the current CSP and exact public-file allowlist. Make the renderer optional: authentication, monitoring and semantic HTML controls must work if WebGL fails. Suspend animation when hidden or out of view. Honor reduced motion. Cap pixel density and frame rate; remove the extra transmission pass on mobile. Dispose GPU resources on navigation. Never turn the decorative scene into fabricated infrastructure telemetry.

Acceptance
Confirm that the canvas renders real geometry and advances between observations; compare assembled and exploded views. Verify pause/resume, page transitions, mobile framing and GPU failure behavior. Inspect real server telemetry after deployment. Ship through the existing PR and automated deployment, with screenshots and test evidence.

Cinematic composition revision
Reference supplied by the user: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mFgRGSOGNPM
The opening 0-5 seconds show a large, close-framed metallic watch and an
exploded movement integrated with the page typography. The tutorial uses
image/video assets for its scroll animation. Those assets are not copied.

For Sentinel, keep the actual articulated Three.js model but make the whole
login screen one scene. A close perspective camera, permanent machined
housing, inner spring-loaded spindle, gear bridges, background support arcs
and recessed hardware give the core a setting. Let the silhouette fill the
left two-thirds while the login remains quiet and readable on the right.
Use one restrained warm metal accent against charcoal and cool steel.

Use the CC0 photographic studio environment from Poly Haven for reflections,
with a procedural lighting fallback. Finish the scene with multisampling,
restrained highlight bloom, shallow depth-dependent blur and fine grain.
Mobile uses fewer samples and omits the depth blur and transmission pass.
Mode changes also move the camera smoothly. Do not equate these browser
rendering techniques with the fidelity of an offline film render.

Verify the full scene at 1280x720, 390x844 and a tablet breakpoint, including
text legibility over geometry, animation pause, viewport switching, missing
HDR behavior, missing renderer behavior and authenticated asset access.

Precision finishing and mechanical timing revision
Separate turned titanium, linear satin brushing, perlage on the movement
plate, polished bevels, dark coated metal and small ruby bearings. Add
derivative-filtered object-space machining to surface roughness and normals;
keep marks subtle enough that the metal still reads as maintained hardware.
Use curved bevel profiles, involute-style gear flanks with cutout spokes,
rolling-element bearings, locking pins and curved case engravings. Contact
shading should clarify small gaps without outlining the whole silhouette.

Anchor the chassis to its case. Use a reversible cam sequence: unlock,
clear the cover, open the iris, raise the carriage, extend the gear carrier
and then present the optic. Closing follows the same path in reverse. Avoid
independent spinning of the structural plates. Gear pairs counter-rotate at
their tooth ratio; the spring changes pitch without stretching its wire.
Manual reversal preserves velocity through a critically damped controller.
Test endpoint clearance, loop continuity, reversal and frame-rate independence.
Batch stationary hardware within each animated part and hide fine physical
grooves at mobile sizes. Verify the actual scene after those optimizations.
